Methodology

Momentum score

Same 13-factor checklist used at index level, applied per stock: EMA stacking, RS vs Nifty 50 (12m & 3m), proximity to 52w high, volume confirmation, 6m return, momentum persistence, trend intensity, ADR. Weighted D×0.5 + W×0.3 + M×0.2.

Rating score

Technical Rating (avg of MA + Oscillator Rating) converted to a -2..+2 scale per timeframe, averaged across Daily/Weekly/Monthly, then rescaled to 0-100.

Composite

Composite = Momentum×0.55 + Rating×0.45. Momentum is weighted slightly higher since it directly encodes trend strength, RS, and volume rather than a discretized label.

Universe & filter

485 of 500 scanned stocks had complete data across both the momentum scan and the ratings feed; incomplete rows were dropped rather than estimated.
